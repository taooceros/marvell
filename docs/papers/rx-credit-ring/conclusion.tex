\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Cache-efficient 100--800\,Gbps receive paths do not require replacing standard
NIC DMA, modifying applications with asynchronous pull APIs, or maintaining
dual-path flow-table controllers. \sys{} unifies on-path SmartNIC elastic
buffering with DDIO-locked standard DMA receive rings and unfenced 64-byte
posted MMIO credit signaling into one $O(1)$-state pipeline. Across 1,723
verified 100GbE runs spanning microbenchmarks, 128\,MiB bursts, 8-core skew and
10\,ms stalls, head-to-head \ceio{} tests, and unmodified Memcached~1.6.45,
\sys{} breaks host-memory conservation with +0.20\,cyc/pkt signaling cost,
0.00\% packet loss, 61--160$\times$ lower burst DRAM traffic, full 100GbE net
PCIe draining, and up to 54\% lower application tail latency.
